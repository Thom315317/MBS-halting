# MBS-Halting v0.3 — claims, anti-claims, and reviewer-2 anticipations

Companion to [`docs/RESULT_CARD_MBS_HALTING_V0_3.md`](RESULT_CARD_MBS_HALTING_V0_3.md). All numbers below are sourced from [`results/belief_repair_hard_3seed_accuracy_compute_v1/aggregate_summary.json`](../results/belief_repair_hard_3seed_accuracy_compute_v1/aggregate_summary.json).

## 1. What we can claim

### 1.1 Compute-efficient short-horizon halting policy on `belief_repair_hard`

On `belief_repair_hard` with the v0.3 protocol (3 seeds, identical losses, identical training budget, identical halting hyperparameters), `mbs_adaptive_halting` reaches **OOD mixed = 0.9948 ± 0.0060** at **expected_steps = 4.52 ± 0.33** message-passing iterations, while the strongest fixed-step baseline `rgcn_repair_stability` reaches **0.9440 ± 0.0030** at fixed `T = 8` and the diagnostic ACT baseline `rgcn_repair_stability_act_warmup_t8` reaches **0.9154 ± 0.0203** at **expected_steps = 7.90 ± 0.37**.

Phrase to use: *MBS-Halting learns a **compute-efficient short-horizon halting policy** on `belief_repair_hard`, halting around 4.5 steps on average while reaching OOD-mixed accuracy strictly above both the fixed-step and the ACT-warmup RGCN baselines.*

### 1.2 The advantage is consistent across the 3 seeds

- `mbs_adaptive_halting` per-seed selected OOD mixed: 0.988, 0.996, 1.000.
- `rgcn_repair_stability` per-seed selected OOD mixed: 0.943, 0.947, 0.941.
- `rgcn_repair_stability_act_warmup_t8` per-seed selected OOD mixed: 0.893, 0.922, 0.932.

In particular, the worst MBS seed (0.988) is above the best RGCN-fixed seed (0.947) and above the best RGCN-ACT-warmup seed (0.932). No seed inversion.

### 1.3 The ACT collapse failure mode in RGCN is not the explanation

The RGCN + ACT-warmup variant exists precisely to control for the ACT collapse documented in earlier diagnostic runs (free RGCN-ACT collapses to step ~4 with `λ_ponder = 0.01`). The warmup variant does **not** collapse (expected_steps = 7.90, well above the floor of 4) but still underperforms MBS by ≈ 8 percentage points OOD mixed. So the MBS advantage is not an artefact of "RGCN-ACT was unstable, of course MBS wins."

## 2. What we cannot claim (yet)

### 2.1 Difficulty-adaptive halting

This card does **not** include a difficulty-controlled probe on `belief_repair_hard`. The v0.3 result speaks to **average** expected steps over the OOD mixed distribution, not to **per-instance** halting decisions correlated with instance difficulty. Anyone wanting to claim "MBS halts longer on harder instances" needs a separate experiment (the `adaptive_halting_probe` family is the right place).

### 2.2 Transfer / general reasoning

Single dataset, single task family. No second-task validation. Any claim of generalization is pre-paper.

### 2.3 Stable attractor dynamics

No attractor analysis, no fixed-point study, no Lyapunov-style measurement.

### 2.4 Architectural superiority of MBS in isolation

MBS-Halting carries 308,860 parameters vs ~176,657 for the RGCN baselines. The accuracy gap is not normalized by parameter count.

## 3. Reviewer 2 objections

> "It's a single-task result."

Yes. The campaign covers exactly one synthetic task. The card states this in §10 of the result card and in §1.1 above.

> "`repair_loss` may leak structural supervision."

Yes, `repair_loss` provides per-node supervision over what the repaired belief should be. This is central to the training signal for both MBS and the RGCN baselines, and may carry information that a strict answer-only baseline would lack. The result card explicitly flags this as **not resolved** and as a required follow-up audit.

> "Halting is compute-efficient but not shown difficulty-adaptive."

Correct. The card explicitly does **not** claim difficulty-adaptive halting (§9). The phrasing used is "compute-efficient short-horizon halting policy."

> "MBS has more parameters than RGCN. The comparison is unfair."

Parameter counts are reported explicitly (308,860 vs 176,657 / 176,754). The compute/accuracy claim does not normalize by parameter count. A reviewer can legitimately argue the gain is partly capacity. We acknowledge this and do not denoise it away.

> "The benchmark is synthetic and may have been co-designed with the model."

Yes. The dataset is generated by the project's own code path ([`mbs/datasets.py`](../mbs/datasets.py)), which means co-design risk cannot be ruled out from this card alone. Transfer to an external dataset is the proper rebuttal and is deferred.

## 4. Honest answers to those objections

| Objection | Honest response |
|---|---|
| Single-task result | Acknowledged. Scope is restricted to `belief_repair_hard`. |
| `repair_loss` leakage | Acknowledged as a real concern. Audit pending. The leakage audit must occur before any stronger claim about MBS as a "reasoner". |
| Halting not shown adaptive | Acknowledged. Phrase used is "compute-efficient short-horizon halting policy". The difficulty probe is the proper experiment for the adaptive claim. |
| Parameter asymmetry | Reported explicitly. The claim is about accuracy *and* expected steps under the same protocol, not about per-parameter efficiency. We do not deflect. |
| Synthetic benchmark | Acknowledged. Transfer to a non-co-designed dataset is the proper rebuttal and is deferred to a future result card. |

## 5. Next experiments, explicitly deferred

These are **not** part of v0.3 and are listed here so they don't quietly get added to the v0.3 narrative:

1. **`repair_loss` leakage audit.** Train each variant with `lambda_repair = 0` and report the OOD mixed delta. If MBS-Halting collapses without `repair_loss`, the v0.3 claim narrows; if RGCN baselines collapse more than MBS, the comparison becomes more interpretable.
2. **Difficulty-controlled halting probe** on `belief_repair_hard` and on `adaptive_halting_probe` with the v0.3 trained checkpoints. This is the proper test of the adaptive halting claim and is the prerequisite for the stronger phrasing.
3. **Transfer to a second task.** Either an externally-defined small reasoning task or a different dataset family. Without a second task, no transfer claim.
4. **Parameter-matched RGCN baseline.** A wider RGCN with `d_model ≈ 130` would equalize parameter count and remove the capacity confound from the v0.3 comparison.
5. **Pure off-the-shelf ACT baseline (no warmup) at λ values that don't collapse.** Sweep `λ_ponder ∈ {1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2}` for RGCN + ACT-no-warmup to find the regime where free ACT is stable on RGCN, and re-do the comparison there.

None of these are required to defend the v0.3 phrasing **provided** the phrasing stays at "compute-efficient short-horizon halting policy" and does not stretch to "adaptive halting" or "general reasoning".
